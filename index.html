What this is

Ratings and forecasts, scored in public

Every Super Netball player is rated against the others at her position on two seasons of club minutes. A club is the best seven it can field. A match forecast is the gap between two clubs, home court added, and it is logged before the game and scored after it, misses included.

What it is not: official, a tipping service, or a model of international netball. The Constellation Cup forecasts are an experiment and are labelled as one.
